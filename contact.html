FAQ

Common Questions

What areas do you service?

We operate throughout South Africa, with active projects in Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Durban, and surrounding areas. For projects outside these regions, we can discuss arrangements.

How much do your services cost?

Our fees depend on project scope and complexity. We offer both percentage-based and fixed-fee arrangements. Contact us for a free consultation and quote tailored to your specific project.

Do you work with specific contractors or can I use my own?

We maintain a network of vetted, qualified contractors, but we're also happy to work with contractors you already know and trust. Our role is to ensure quality delivery regardless of who does the work.

What size projects do you handle?

We manage projects ranging from small renovations (R500K+) to large commercial developments (R25M+). No project is too small or too large for our professional attention.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary significantly based on project scope. A residential renovation might take 2-3 months, while a new commercial build could take 12-18 months. We'll provide detailed timeline estimates during consultation.
