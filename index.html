FAQ

Common Questions

We have a rigorous vetting process that includes background checks, license verification, insurance validation, reference checks, and portfolio review. Only contractors who meet our strict quality standards become part of our network.
We manage residential, commercial, and industrial projects ranging from renovations and extensions to new builds and large-scale developments. Our network includes specialists in each category to ensure the right fit for your project.
We provide regular progress reports, photo updates, and milestone tracking. You'll have direct access to your project dashboard where you can monitor timelines, budgets, and communications in real-time.
Our fees are transparent and project-based, typically ranging from 5-10% of the total project value depending on complexity and scope. We provide detailed quotes upfront with no hidden costs.
Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A typical bathroom renovation might take 2-3 weeks, while a custom home build could take 8-12 months. We provide detailed timelines during the consultation phase.
